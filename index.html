The foundation
for success

Interns step into the same rooms where today's biggest records are made, learning directly from the standards and systems that drive world-class productions. From session prep and client etiquette to mixing workflows and studio discipline, to the business side of music — scheduling, invoicing, label communications, and brand operations — this program gives you hands-on training across the creative and administrative sides of a Grammy Award-winning studio. You won't just learn how records are made; you'll understand how the business around them runs.
